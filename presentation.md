- AI is projected to add **$1.7 Trillion** to India's GDP by **2035**

  - Healthcare

# Healthcare Case Study
- **The Problem:** 1 Doctor per 1,456 people (WHO says 1:1000)
- **The AI Solution:**
  - Diagnostic support for rural doctors
  - AI-based TB & Cancer screening (Radiology)
  - Telemedicine with vernacular NLP

---

# Agriculture: The Backbone of Bharat
- **Predictive Analytics:** Forecasting demand before sowing
- **Yield Optimization:** Soil health and pest detection
- **Market Access:** AI for better price discovery for small farmers

---

# 🚀 Live Demo: Crop Predictor
- **What it does:** Uses historical demand/price data to guide farmers.
- **Tech stack:** Node.js, Express, Data Insights.
- **Real Impact:** Preventing "Glut" situations where prices crash due to oversupply.

> **Speaker Note:** Briefly show the dashboard. Explain that even simple logic can save a farmer's livelihood.
